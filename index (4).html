About Me

I'm a 2nd-year B.Tech Mechanical Engineering student at Madhav Institute of Technology & Science, Gwalior — passionate about drone engineering, structural simulation, and pushing the limits of lightweight design.

As CAD Designer for Team Trinetra UAV, I lead the 3D modeling and FEA simulation of our competition drone. My work spans SolidWorks parametric modeling, ANSYS Workbench structural analysis (static, modal, buckling, fatigue), and carbon fiber composite design for our 8 kg class quadcopter.

Currently building towards SUAS 2026 — a national-level autonomous UAS competition themed around disaster relief. Open to internships, research collaborations, and drone projects.
